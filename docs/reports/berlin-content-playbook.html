Berlin Biryani Content Playbook

What to post, on which platform, in which language, for which community and on which day. Built from current platform research and from the platform's own data (36,324 TikTok videos from 506 Berlin restaurants so far, with Reddit and YouTube collection running).

Every number here lives in the Postgres platform (marts.*, ml.feature_content) and refreshes weekly. Research sources are stored with each rule in core.content_priors.

The short answer

  1. Content beats clock. Across 36k Berlin restaurant TikToks, posting time between 09:00 and 22:00 changes performance by less than ±3 % against the account's own average. Topic, hook and format matter far more. Only 04:00–08:00 is clearly weak.
  2. Feast moments win. Ramadan content performs about 1.5× an account's usual video (+0.42 log-lift, 168 videos from 32 accounts). Price and value angles (+0.28) and openings or news (+0.30) also lift. Plain recipes underperform (−0.24).
  3. Speak to each community in its own dish. Berlin has 107k Turkish, 112k East Slavic, 50k Indian, 41k Syrian, 32k Vietnamese and 25k Afghan citizens. Each has its own meat-and-rice feast (etli pilav, plov, biryani, maqluba, cơm tấm, Kabuli palaw). Bridge from their dish to kacchi, with their greeting in their script.
  4. One video, many cuts. Shoot the dum seal breaking and the rice pull once. Cut 15–59 s versions for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, a carousel for Instagram, a text post for Threads, and a long making-of for YouTube. Never repost with another platform's watermark: Instagram, Facebook and TikTok all demote it.
  5. Plan by occasion, not by week. Start teasers 10–14 days before Eid, Ramadan, Nowruz, Diwali, Durga Puja and Lunar New Year. The calendar below has the dates, greetings and lead times.

Platform by platform

Times are Berlin clock time. Industry studies (Sprout Social, Buffer, Socialinsider, Dash Social, 2025–26) are a starting point. Our own data replaces them as it grows. "Data" shows what the platform can collect today.

TikTok

Discovery engine for under-35s; the main reach channel
Format
Vertical video 15–59 s (best in our data: 15–29 s +0.07, 30–59 s +0.04; under 15 s −0.05). Hook inside 2 s: average watch time is 3.75 s.
Angles
Ramadan / iftar, price per portion, "new in Berlin", halal, spicy challenge, dum-pot reveal, staff and family story.
Text
Say the dish and "Berlin" out loud and on screen: TikTok search reads speech and captions. 6–10 hashtags did best (+0.11); 1–3 did worst (−0.15).
Cadence
3–5 per week; more than 4 a day cuts engagement 28 %.
Avoid
Watermarked reposts, low-effort AI video, recipe-only clips.
Data
public profiles ✓ 36k videos; food creators being added

Instagram

Brand home; reach via Reels, depth via carousels
Format
Reels up to 90 s for reach (+36 % reach vs carousels). Carousels for saves and engagement (+12 % vs Reels): menu, price, "which kacchi are you".
Signal
Sends per reach (DM shares) is the top signal for non-followers. Make "send this to your friend" content.
Text
At most 5 hashtags (hard limit since Dec 2025). Keyword captions: posts are indexed by Google.
When
Tue–Thu, 12:00–19:00 per studies; Sunday weakest.
Cadence
3–5 posts per week, plus Stories daily around lunch and dinner.
Data

YouTube

Search and long-term proof: Shorts plus one long film a month
Format
Shorts with original audio (copyrighted music over 60 s gets blocked). Long-form gets about 6× the views of Shorts: "40 kg of kacchi for Eid", "Old Dhaka recipe in Berlin".

The title carries the keyword ("Kacchi Biryani Berlin"). The first 3 hashtags show above the title.
When
Shorts Fri 16:00; long-form Sun 10:00.

Threads

Conversation, local Berlin food talk

Short text with one photo. Ask a question ("Kacchi or tehari for Friday?"). Replies and early velocity drive reach. Links are not penalised.
Text
One topic tag per post, e.g. "Berlin Food".
When
Weekday mornings 07:00–12:00; daily.
Data

Reddit

Trust channel: r/berlin, r/berlinsocialclub, r/biryani
Rule
Never advertise. Join "where to eat biryani in Berlin" threads as the owner and say so. Keep at least 9 helpful comments for each mention of your own place.
Works
An honest "we cook Old Dhaka kacchi in Berlin, ask us anything" post, Ramadan iftar tips, photos of the pot.
Data
archive ✓ 31 subreddits since 2019, collecting now

The dataset behind this

  • Posts, every platform (core.post, core.post_metrics_snapshot): TikTok 36k, Reddit 34k and growing (31 subreddits since 2019), YouTube once the key is in, Instagram after Meta review, own posts 417 (Instagram, Facebook, Threads).
  • Tags per post (core.content_tag, ops.text_language): the language (42 detected), dishes (107 community dishes plus 952 Wikidata rice dishes in 44 languages), communities, occasions, and 17 format cues (review, price, ranking, ASMR, dum reveal, behind the scenes…).
  • Audiences (core.community_audience): 38 communities by Berlin total, district and LOR planning area, half-yearly since Dec 2025.
  • Occasions (core.occasion_day): 2015–2030 for 38 communities with greeting, tone, lead time, iftar and suhoor times, plus Berlin festivals and Christmas markets.
  • Trends (marts.topic_trends_weekly, core.interest_daily): weekly topic momentum per platform, and daily Wikipedia interest since 2015 for 100+ dishes and occasions in 27 languages.
  • ML table (ml.feature_content, exported by mip ml export content_features): 69,898 posts with leakage-free features and three labels (lift vs the account's earlier posts, percentile, viral top 10 %).

Collected from public data and official APIs only: no logins to others' accounts, no private groups, no CAPTCHA tricks. Commenter and author names are stored as salted hashes.
